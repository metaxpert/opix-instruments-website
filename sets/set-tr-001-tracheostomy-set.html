Tracheostomy Set

SET-TR-001 · Airway Surgery

An airway tray for elective and emergency tracheostomy, with tracheal dilators and hooks, cricoid hook, retraction for the strap muscles and the fine haemostasis the neck requires. German pattern, AISI 410/420 stainless steel, passivated and inspected against master pattern, kitted in a lidded perforated tray.
