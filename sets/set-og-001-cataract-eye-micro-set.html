Cataract / Eye Micro Set

SET-OG-001 · Ophthalmic Microsurgery

A micro-ophthalmic tray for extracapsular and manual small- incision cataract surgery: speculum, Castroviejo calipers and needle holders, capsulorhexis and tying forceps, Vannas and Westcott scissors, lens loop and vectis, iris and Sinskey hooks and irrigation cannulas, in a silicone-lined micro-instrument case. German pattern, AISI 420/440 and titanium where noted, passivated and inspected under magnification.
