Thoracotomy Set

SET-CV-001 · Thoracic Surgery

An open-chest tray for posterolateral thoracotomy, lobectomy and pleural procedures: Finochietto rib spreader, rib shears and raspatories, lung-grasping forceps, bronchus and vascular clamps, long dissecting and suture instruments, and deep retraction, on a laparotomy core. German pattern, AISI 410/420 stainless steel, passivated and inspected against master pattern.
