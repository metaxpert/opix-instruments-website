About Opix Instruments

Opix Instruments is a manufacturer and exporter of surgical and medical instruments based in Sialkot, Pakistan — the city that supplies the majority of the world's hand-held surgical instruments. Our lines cover scalpels and knives, forceps, scissors, needle holders, retractors and complete procedure sets, produced in forged surgical stainless steel to German patterns.

Quality & regulatory

ISO 13485Certified quality management system for medical device manufacturing, covering material control, process validation and final inspection.
CE MarkingInstruments carry CE marking with supporting technical documentation for the European market.
FDA RegisteredUS FDA establishment registration for export to the United States.
MembershipsSurgical Instrument Manufacturers Association of Pakistan (SIMAP) and Sialkot Chamber of Commerce & Industry (SCCI).

OEM & private label

We develop instruments under our buyers' own brands: custom laser etching, bespoke packaging, pattern development from samples or drawings, and mixed-container consolidation. Contact us with your specification for a development quotation.
