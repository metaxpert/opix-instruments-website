Opix manufactures the complete scalpel and surgical knife line in German-pattern martensitic stainless steel — solid and fluted handles in sizes #3, #4, #5 and #7, matching blade holders, and operating, amputation, resection and post-mortem knives. Every handle is forged, hardened to specification and hand-finished, with catalogue numbers that cross-reference the standard international patterns distributors already stock.
